A parametric birch-plywood bookshelf, rendered live in 3D. Drag the sliders (or type exact numbers) and the model rebuilds instantly. Drag the scene to orbit, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan.

Sheets (2440 × 1220 mm)

The PDF is a two-page sheet: 3D view, dimensioned elevations and readout, then the cut list. The CSV is the cut list alone.

Rendered with three.js (pinned r186, vendored in vendor/three/); PDF export with jsPDF (pinned 4.2.1, vendored in vendor/jspdf/) — no CDN dependencies. Carcass: sides full height and depth, notched at the back-bottom to clear skirting; bottom shelf sits on a plinth zone with a set-back front rail; top, bottom flush with the side fronts; internal shelves set back by the same amount as the plinth rail; optional 6 mm back rebated into the sides, top and bottom (lap ¾ of the ply thickness, rebate 1 mm deeper than the back ply). Weight estimate uses 680 kg/m³ for birch ply. The 3D model, the readout, the sheet estimate and the downloadable cut list all derive from the same panel rows, so they cannot disagree.
