Rendered with three.js (pinned r186, vendored in vendor/three/ — no CDN dependency). Carcass: sides full height and depth, notched at the back-bottom to clear skirting; bottom shelf sits on a plinth zone with a set-back front rail; top, bottom flush with the side fronts; internal shelves set back by the same amount as the plinth rail; optional 6 mm back inset within the frame. Weight estimate uses 680 kg/m³ for birch ply.
