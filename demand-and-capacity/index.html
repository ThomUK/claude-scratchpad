A trust-wide, specialty-connected demand & capacity model for Nottingham University Hospitals, built to answer: what capacity is needed to meet the NHS constitutional standards — RTT (65% by Apr 2027, 80% by Apr 2028, 92% by Apr 2029), cancer, diagnostics (DM01) and urgent care. Phase 1: the elective (RTT) spine — waiting list → required clock stops → outpatient, theatre, bed and diagnostic capacity, per treatment function. Method: Fong et al. (2022) / NHSRwaitinglist queueing theory.

Waiting list, performance, referral demand, clock stops and admitted share are seeded from the published NHSE RTT full extracts (April 2026 position; April 2024/2025 for calibration). Growth and removals are calibrated from the pre-EPR pair (Apr-24→Apr-25) — the EPR-era year is recoding-contaminated. Operational parameters (day-case rates, cases/session, N:FU, LOS) remain estimates. Every figure is editable; nothing here is operational advice.
