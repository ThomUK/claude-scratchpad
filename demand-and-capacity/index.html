A trust-wide, specialty-connected demand & capacity model for Nottingham University Hospitals, built to answer: what capacity is needed to meet the NHS constitutional standards — RTT (65% by Apr 2027, 80% by Apr 2028, 92% by Apr 2029), cancer, diagnostics (DM01) and urgent care. All five phases are live — this page is the elective (RTT) spine (waiting list → required clock stops → outpatient, theatre, bed and diagnostic capacity, per treatment function); diagnostics, cancer, UEC and workforce are linked above. Method: Fong et al. (2022) / NHSRwaitinglist queueing theory.

Waiting list, performance, referral demand, clock stops and admitted share are seeded from the published NHSE RTT full extracts (April 2026 position; April 2024/2025 for calibration). Growth and removals are calibrated from the pre-EPR pair (Apr-24→Apr-25) — the EPR-era year is recoding-contaminated. Operational parameters (day-case rates, cases/session, N:FU, LOS) remain estimates. The census shape factor k (… per TFC) anchors implied performance to each TFC's published %<18wk and, by default, is held at today's value to 2030 — k reflects booking discipline, the sustainable list scales linearly with it, and the booking-discipline-drift lever tests the assumption (see the k explainer). Every figure is editable; nothing here is operational advice.
