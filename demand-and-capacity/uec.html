Seeded from the published Monthly A&E (MSitAE) provider CSVs (April 2024/25/26) and UEC Daily SitRep winter timeseries (2023-24 → 2025-26). From Apr-26 the co-located UTC activity reports under RX1's code — all-types performance jumps for reporting reasons; type-1 is the comparable ED series. Growth is calibrated from the latest pair (the earliest is contaminated by the UTC split — the reverse of the RTT/DM01 EPR situation). 4-hour milestones (78% Apr-27 → 86% Apr-28 → 95% Apr-29) and the 12-hour-to-zero date are modelling assumptions. The shared bed pool — where UEC meets the elective recovery

Emergency occupied beds follow Little's Law (daily admissions × LOS); open beds needed add the occupancy-target headroom. The elective spine's bed requirement (phase 1) is drawn on the same axis — both press on one adult G&A pool of ~1,600 beds.

The long-stay columns are the discharge-delay proxy: beds held by patients in hospital over 7/14/21 days. Releasing long-stay beds is the biggest single lever on the pool.

Ambulance handover (data gap)

Trust-level handover delays (>30/>60 minutes) are published in a separate NHSE ambulance data collection, not in the sitrep editions loaded here. National reporting has repeatedly identified EMAS handovers at NUH as pressured; the mechanism is the one this page models — handover delay is queueing at the front door for the same beds the long-stay and elective columns are holding. Upload the handover extract and this panel becomes data-driven.
