Phase 4: the A&E 4-hour standard (a flow standard, like cancer — each month's attendances scored on timeliness), 12-hour decision-to-admit waits, and the emergency bed base — where UEC meets the elective spine: average occupied emergency beds = daily admissions × length of stay (Little's Law a third time), and the elective recovery's bed requirement stacks on the same adult G&A pool.

Seeded from the published Monthly A&E (MSitAE) provider CSVs (April 2024/25/26) and UEC Daily SitRep winter timeseries (2023-24 → 2025-26). From Apr-26 the co-located UTC activity reports under RX1's code — all-types performance jumps for reporting reasons; type-1 is the comparable ED series. Growth is calibrated from the latest pair (the earliest is contaminated by the UTC split — the reverse of the RTT/DM01 EPR situation). Emergency flows run 7 days a week, so growth pairs compare CALENDAR months — all three Aprils have 30 days and 22 weekdays — and no working-day adjustment applies (unlike the elective modules). 4-hour milestones (82% Apr-27 per the Medium Term Planning Framework objective → 88% Apr-28 → 95% Apr-29) and the 12-hour-to-zero date are documented modelling assumptions; the glide is also derived for type-1, since the all-types metric is UTC-flattered. Ambulance handover is seeded from the NHSE Ambulance Handover Times by Acute Trust collection (monthly timeseries from Oct-2023 + April snapshots).

The shared bed pool — where UEC meets the elective recovery

Emergency occupied beds follow Little's Law (daily admissions × LOS, with LOS derived from winter occupancy net of the elective share so the decomposition doesn't double-count); open beds needed add the occupancy-target headroom. The elective spine's bed requirement (phase 1) stacks on top — both press on one adult G&A pool of ~1,600 beds.
