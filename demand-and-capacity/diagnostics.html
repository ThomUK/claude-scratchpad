Phase 2: modality-level diagnostic waiting lists against the 6-week DM01 standard (interim ambition 95% under 6 weeks; constitutional <1% over). Same queueing method as the elective spine, on a 6-week window: required tests per month = demand + phased clearance to the sustainable list size, with census-shape calibration per modality.

Seeded from the published DM01 provider files (April 2026 position; April 2024/2025 for demand-growth calibration — pre-EPR pair used). Demand is estimated as waiting-list tests + year-on-year list change; April activity is bank-holiday depressed. Milestones (95% by Apr-27, 99% by Apr-29) are a modelling assumption aligned to the RTT trajectory.
