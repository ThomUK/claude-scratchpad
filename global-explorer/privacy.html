Global Explorer

Privacy policy

Global Explorer · Last updated 22 November 2026

In short: Global Explorer does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no adverts and no analytics. Your progress stays on your own device.

Who this covers

This policy covers the Global Explorer app for Android, the schools' web version and this website, globalexplorer.app. All are made by the same independent developer, referred to here as "we".

What stays on your device

To remember where you are in the game, Global Explorer saves a small amount of information in the app's own storage on your device (or, for the web version, in your browser):

  • your settings, such as the level you play and kilometres or miles;
  • your progress: the passport stamps you have earned and your scores;
  • the countries you have marked as places you have been.

This information never leaves your device and is never sent to us or to anyone else. You can delete your stamps and scores at any time with Settings → Reset passport and progress, or delete everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

What we do not collect

  • We do not ask for your name, email address or any other personal details, and there is no sign-in.
  • We do not use your location. The globe shows the whole world and does not know where you are.
  • We do not show adverts and do not use advertising identifiers.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services, and the app contains no third-party code that collects data.

The Android app lists internet access among its permissions because that is standard for apps built this way. Global Explorer itself makes no internet requests: all of its maps and country information are built into the app, and it works offline.

Purchases

Where Global Explorer offers in-app purchases, they are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment card details. Google shares limited order information with us, such as an order number, the item bought and the country of purchase, which we use only to confirm purchases, give refunds and answer questions about an order. Google's own privacy policy covers the payment itself: policies.google.com/privacy.

This website and the schools' version

globalexplorer.app is hosted by Cloudflare. Like most web hosts, Cloudflare processes technical details of each visit, such as IP addresses, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse; we do not have access to records of individual visits. Cloudflare's privacy policy explains what it does: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

The schools' web version asks for the access code we give each school. Once the code is entered, the site stores one cookie on the device so that it does not ask again; the cookie records only which school's code was used and when, and nothing about the person using it. The site needs this cookie to work, and sets no other cookies. The web version also keeps a copy of the game's files in the browser so that it works offline.

Children

Global Explorer is designed to be suitable for children. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. Schools using the web version do not need to give us any information about their pupils.

Changes to this policy

If the way Global Explorer handles information changes, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
